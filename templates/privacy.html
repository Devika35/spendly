Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

Last updated: October 2026

01Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and a securely hashed version of your password. We never store your password in plain text.

When you use Spendly, we store the expenses you enter — amounts, categories, dates and any descriptions you add. We do not connect to your bank or collect card or account numbers.

02How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to run Spendly for you: signing you in, showing your expenses, and generating your spending summaries and category breakdowns.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to build marketing profiles.

03Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and kept for as long as your account is active. We take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

You can ask us to delete your account at any time, and we will remove your personal data and expense records.

04Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. Apart from essential hosting and infrastructure providers, we do not share your data with third parties.

If we add new third-party services in the future, we will update this policy to describe them.

05Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to access or delete your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.app.
